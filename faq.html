Good to know

Questions, answered

A few of the things people ask me most about working together.

What does The Gemini Social do?

I manage Instagram and Facebook for small businesses. That covers strategy, content calendars, turning your photos and videos into posts and reels, captions in your brand's voice, and a monthly results report.

Who do you work with?

I specialise in wellness and wellbeing businesses, and I work with small businesses of all kinds that care about showing up well online. My clients include fitness and Pilates studios, a business coach and property businesses.

Do you work with clients outside the Sunshine Coast?

Yes. I'm based on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, and work with businesses anywhere, including clients in New Zealand and the United States.

How does it work?

It starts with a free discovery call. From there you fill in an onboarding form, we have a strategy call, and I create your content calendar and posts. Nothing goes live until you've approved it.

Do I need to provide photos and videos?

Yes. I work with your own images and videos and edit them into finished content. If you're a Sunshine Coast business, we can also talk about me creating content for you in person.

What do your packages include?

Every package is tailored to the business. You can see the starting points on the Packages page, and we shape the details together on your discovery call.

Still have a question?

I'm always happy to chat. Book a free discovery call or send me a message, and I'll reply personally.
