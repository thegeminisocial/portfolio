      A little about me

      Two heads are better than one

      Hi, I'm Marina. I've spent around five years working across social media, radio and podcasting, and my favourite part has always been the same: helping a good business get the attention it deserves.

      Most owners I work with are brilliant at what they do and completely stretched for time. Social media becomes the job that happens at 10pm, or not at all. That's where I come in.

      Think of it as strategy and creativity, you and me, side by side. You bring the heart of your business, I bring the plan, the content and the consistency. I'm based on the Sunshine Coast and work with businesses across Australia and New Zealand.
