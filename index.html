A little about me

Two heads are better than one

Hi, I'm Marina, founder of The Gemini Social. As a true Gemini, communication is at the heart of everything I do. My background spans social media, radio and podcasting, so telling stories that make people stop and listen comes naturally to me.

I'm curious, genuinely interested in people and energised by the stories behind the businesses I work with. The Gemini Social was built on one simple belief: social media works best when it feels real.

That's why I work closely with a small number of clients at a time, getting to know your business, your voice and your goals well enough to create content that actually sounds and looks like you. Think of it as strategy and creativity, you and me, side by side. If you want someone who'll treat your business with the same care you do, you're in the right place.

I specialise in wellness and wellbeing businesses and work with small businesses of all kinds, from the Sunshine Coast to clients worldwide.
